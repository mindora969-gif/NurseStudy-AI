Quick revision method

20-minute active recall cycle

  1. Read one small topic for 7 minutes.
  2. Close the notes and write what you remember for 5 minutes.
  3. Check gaps and correct errors for 5 minutes.
  4. Explain the topic aloud in 3 minutes.
