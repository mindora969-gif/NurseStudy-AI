Learn the anatomy

Right atrium & ventricleReceive oxygen-poor blood and send it toward the lungs through pulmonary circulation.

Left atrium & ventricleReceive oxygen-rich blood from the lungs. The left ventricle pumps blood into the aorta.

ValvesTricuspid, pulmonary, mitral and aortic valves help keep blood flowing in one direction.

Major vesselsVenae cavae, pulmonary arteries, pulmonary veins and aorta connect the heart with the body and lungs.
