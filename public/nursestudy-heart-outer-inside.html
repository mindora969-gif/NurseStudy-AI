Learn the anatomy

Right atrium & ventricleReceive oxygen-poor blood from the body; the right ventricle pumps it toward the lungs.
Left atrium & ventricleReceive oxygen-rich blood from the lungs; the left ventricle pumps it into the aorta.
ValvesTricuspid, pulmonary, mitral and aortic valves help keep blood flowing in one direction.
Septum & major vesselsThe septum separates the two sides. The venae cavae, pulmonary vessels and aorta connect the heart to the body and lungs.
